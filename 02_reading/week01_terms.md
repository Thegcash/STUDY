# Week 1 reading: Modern Robotics Ch. 1 + G1 overview

- **Degrees of freedom (DOF)**: the number of independent ways a joint can move. My elbow only bends (1 DOF); my shoulder goes up/down, forward/back and rotates (3 DOF). The G1 has 23.
- **IMU**: a chip in the torso with an accelerometer (feels acceleration and which way is down) and a gyroscope (feels tilting and turning). It's how a humanoid keeps its balance.
- **Links and joints**: links are the rigid pieces, joints connect them and let them move. Combine them in different ways and you get different robots.
- **Locomotion**: how a robot moves itself from place to place, like walking on two legs.
- **World models**: an AI's internal picture of how the physical world works, so it can predict what will happen if it takes an action.
