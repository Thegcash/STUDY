# Week 1 reading: Modern Robotics Ch. 1 + G1 overview

- **Degrees of freedom (DOF)**: the number of independent ways a joint can move. My elbow only bends (1 DOF); my shoulder goes up/down, left/right and rotates (3 DOF).
- **IMU**: a chip in every humanoid that helps it balance. It has an accelerometer (feels acceleration) and a gyroscope (feels tilting and turning).
- **Links and joints**: links are the rigid pieces, joints connect them. You can arrange them in different ways to build completely different robots.
- **World models**: an AI's understanding of the physical world and physics, so it can predict what will happen after an action.
- **Locomotion**: how a robot moves itself from place to place, like walking.
